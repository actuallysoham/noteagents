Announcements

Updates from the organizers for participating teams, newest first.

  1. Nov 24, 2026

    Harnyx announces $5k in awards and $100 credits

    Harnyx, which offers a deep research API for AI agents, has announced $5k in credit awards in response to the competition: $3k, $1.5k and $500 for the 1st, 2nd and 3rd place teams building on Harnyx. Harnyx is also offering a $100 credit to every WSDM Cup team. To maintain eligibility for the bonus prizes, Harnyx has augmented their API to allow restricting the research backend by model size.

  2. Nov 19, 2026

    Sensitive content in post feeds

    Eligible post feeds are not filtered for adult or graphic content. If you would like to skip these posts, request the possibly_sensitive field via tweet.fields and drop posts where it is true. The flag is set by the post author or by X, and only on posts with identified sensitive media or links, so it may not catch all sensitive content. If your note writer sends post contents or media to a model, check that provider’s content policy and consider adding your own filter.

  3. Nov 13, 2026

    Parallel announces $5k finalist awards

    Parallel has announced $5k credit awards in response to the competition for finalist agents built on Parallel! Parallel offers infrastructure to support agents using the web, and includes a free tier.
